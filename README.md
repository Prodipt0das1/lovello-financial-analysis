# Working Capital & Financial Performance Analysis of Lovello Ice-Cream PLC

**Academic group project | Managerial Finance (FIN 3123) | United International University | 2026**

**Study period:** FY2020–21 to FY2024–25 (five fiscal years).

## Overview
An academic case study of working capital management at Taufika Foods and Lovello Ice-Cream PLC. The project examines liquidity, asset management, operating and cash conversion cycles, and model-estimated additional funds needed (AFN).

## Methods
- Comparative financial statement analysis
- Liquidity and working capital efficiency ratios
- Current asset investment and financing policy analysis
- Days inventory outstanding (DIO), days sales outstanding (DSO), days payables outstanding (DPO)
- Operating cycle (OC) and cash conversion cycle (CCC)
- AFN estimation and sensitivity to sales growth, profit margin, earnings retention, and capital intensity

## Selected findings from the academic report
| Metric | FY2020–21 | FY2024–25 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current ratio | 2.18 | 2.50 |
| Quick ratio | 1.43 | 1.80 |
| Cash ratio | 0.43 | 0.17 |
| DIO (days, rounded) | 221 | 319 |
| DSO (days, rounded) | 128 | 184 |
| Operating cycle (days, rounded) | 349 | 503 |
| Working capital turnover | 1.80 | 0.92 |
| Estimated AFN (BDT millions) | -256.4 | 408.8 |

**Interpretation:** Reported liquidity ratios were comparatively strong, but longer inventory and receivables cycles suggested reduced working capital efficiency. AFN results are model estimates, **not evidence of actual funds borrowed**.
