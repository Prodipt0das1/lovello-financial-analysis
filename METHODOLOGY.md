# Methodology and review checklist

## Data and period
Historical financial statements for FY2020–21 through FY2024–25 as analyzed in the Managerial Finance group coursework. Cite the exact annual report titles, pages and URLs after verifying them.

## Measures
- Current ratio = Current assets / Current liabilities
- Quick ratio = (Current assets - Inventory) / Current liabilities (verify workbook definition)
- Cash ratio = Cash and cash equivalents / Current liabilities
- DIO = Average inventory / Cost of goods sold × 365
- DSO = Average accounts receivable / Sales × 365
- DPO = Average accounts payable / Cost of goods sold × 365
- OC = DIO + DSO
- CCC = DIO + DSO - DPO
- AFN = (A0/S0)ΔS - (L0/S0)ΔS - (PM × S1 × retention ratio)
